About & Legal

This site does not host, upload or store any movies.

Free Classics

Each film in Free Classics is in the public domain in the United States, either because its copyright was never renewed or because it was released without a valid copyright notice. They play through the Internet Archive's official embed player. Public-domain status can differ outside the U.S.

Trailers and movie info

Movie details and posters come from TMDB. Trailers play through YouTube's official embedded player and only use videos TMDB lists for that movie.

Where to watch

"Where to watch" shows only licensed services, such as Netflix, Kanopy, hoopla and Tubi. Some need a subscription or a library card. This site never links to unlicensed streaming sites.

Privacy

No ads, pop-ups, trackers or accounts. Your list and settings stay in this browser.
